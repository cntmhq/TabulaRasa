Create an SVG logo for a project called "engram". The logo should be styled as a personal contract signature — the kind of confident, flowing autograph
someone scrawls at the bottom of a legal document. Think of the signature at the end of a will, contract, or formal letter of withdrawal.

Conceptual context (to inform the style, not to be depicted literally):
Engram is a digital tool for revoking consent and opting out of data collection — essentially a "digital signature revocation" instrument 
that helps users formally withdraw permissions they previously gave to data brokers under GDPR. The logo should feel like the act of signing 
your name to take back control of your data — a definitive, personal mark of authority. The handwritten signature aesthetic reinforces that 
this is a legally meaningful, human-asserted action.

Style requirements:
  - Handwritten cursive / autograph signature aesthetic — like the kind of stylized name someone has practiced signing on contracts and cheques
  - Confident, fluid, slightly assertive strokes — not timid or decorative
  - Single continuous flowing line where possible, with natural pen-lift variations and ink-weight inconsistencies
  - Should feel like ink applied with a real pen — slight imperfections, no robotic precision
  - Lowercase preferred — matches the personal, handwritten feel

Color palette (dark cyberpunk / cosmic-slate theme):
  - Primary stroke color: #52d1b8 (mint teal)
  - Optional accent / glow highlight: #63ffd7 (brighter mint)
  - Designed to sit on a dark background: #060709 (near-black charcoal)

Strict constraints:
  - Do NOT include any slogan, tagline, descriptor, subtitle, or secondary text — just the single word "engram"
  - No icons, no decorative borders, no boxes around the word, no shield or lock motifs
  - No additional shapes (circles, arrows, etc.) unless they are an organic part of the signature stroke itself (e.g. the terminal flourish under the word)
  - Output must be clean, valid, self-contained SVG markup (no external fonts — convert text to paths/strokes so it renders identically everywhere)
  - Optimize for ~32–64px header height while remaining legible at larger sizes
  - viewBox should be tight to the wordmark with minimal padding
